Privacy Policy

At Qasmani Software Solutions, we take user privacy seriously. Here is a summary of our practices:

  • We do not collect personal information unless explicitly required for app functionality.
  • We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
  • Our apps may use third-party services (such as Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics) that collect anonymous usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.
  • Users can contact us at any time to request data deletion.

For app-specific privacy policies, please refer to the individual app listing on Google Play Store. If you have questions, reach out via the contact form below.
