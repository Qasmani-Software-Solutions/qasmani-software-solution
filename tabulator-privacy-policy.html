Privacy Policy

App: Tabulator — Calculator with Tabs & History  ·  Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Tabulator is a calculator app developed by Qasmani Software Solutions. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

Tabulator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All data you enter — calculations, workspace names, and history — is stored exclusively on your device.

Data Storage

  • All calculation history and workspace data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database.
  • No data is sent to any server or third-party service.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data.

Internet Access

Tabulator does not require internet access to function. The app may request internet access solely to process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. No personal data is transmitted during this process.

In-App Purchases

Tabulator offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") processed entirely through Google Play. We do not handle or store any payment information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how purchase data is handled.

Third-Party Services

Tabulator currently does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Tabulator does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
